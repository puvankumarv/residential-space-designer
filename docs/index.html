What it is

A design method Claude follows, not a black box.

A skill is a folder of instructions, reference rules and scripts that Claude loads when a task needs it. Nothing is "trained": the quality comes from the written rules, and every number cites where it came from.

Plain chat

Ideas, no guarantees

General inspiration. Nobody checks clearances, headroom or whether a wardrobe door can actually open.

This skill

A method plus a checker

A fixed procedure, room-by-room rule modules, a layout format, and a Python checker that flags unsafe or unusable designs before you see them.

Next: MCP + Blender

Tools that render

How it works

From "my room is a mess" to a checked plan.

Describe

Any mix of inputs. Claude asks at most five focused questions for what's missing.

  • Text or voice transcript
  • Room photos or a walkthrough video
  • A floor plan

Diagnose & decide

It names the real failures, then makes the room's master decision and says why the alternatives lose.

  • Bedroom: how people sleep
  • Kitchen: galley, L, U or island
  • Bathroom: fixture layout

Lay out & check

A layout JSON per room, run through the checker. Every error gets fixed before anything is shown.

  • Collisions, door swings, clearances
  • Room-specific code checks

Deliver

Everything needed to build it, or render it.

  • Brief and zone plan
  • Floor plan and bill of materials
  • Safety notes and a reveal script

The checker

Every layout is tested before you see it.

check_layout.py is plain Python with no dependencies. It reads a layout JSON, prints errors and warnings with their source, and draws an ASCII floor plan.

a deliberately broken bathroom
$ python3 scripts/check_layout.py bath.json
ERROR: toilet: 0.00 m clear in front of toilet < 0.53 m [IRC-P]
ERROR: toilet: centreline to left obstruction 0.30 m < 0.38 m [IRC-P]
ERROR: vanity: 0.00 m clear in front of basin < 0.53 m [IRC-P]
ERROR: shower: shower 0.70×0.92 m below 0.76×0.76 m / 0.58 m² [IRC-P]
4 error(s), 0 warning(s)

$ python3 scripts/check_layout.py examples/compact-bathroom.json
0 error(s), 0 warning(s)

Worked example

Twins, 11 m², and no more bunk-bed fights.

Instead of a stacked bunk, the upper bed turns the corner over a desk and wardrobe. Dashed outlines are at loft level. The reveal script below doubles as the camera path for a walkthrough video.

Reveal script

  1. Two kids, one 11 m² room. No more bunk-bed fights over who gets the top.
  2. The upper bed turns the corner instead of stacking, so both kids can sit up in bed.
  3. Every step is a drawer, seven of them, with anti-slip treads.
  4. Under the loft there's a full desk with 1.55 m of headroom, plus a sliding wardrobe.
  5. The lower bed keeps open sky above it, with drawers that roll out instead of lifting.
  6. The window becomes the second desk, with the best daylight in the room.
  7. Sliding doors mean the wardrobe opens fully even in a tight room.
  8. The centre stays open for play. Same storage, same desk space, one each.
Install & use

Up and running in two minutes.

  1. Clone the repository 
    git clone https://github.com/puvankumarv/residential-space-designer.git
  2. Copy the skill into your skills folder (use .claude/skills/ inside a project to scope it to that project) 
    cp -r residential-space-designer/residential-space-designer ~/.claude/skills/
  3. Start Claude Code and describe your space. The skill loads automatically when the request matches.
  1. Download the repository as a ZIP from GitHub and unzip it. 
    https://github.com/puvankumarv/residential-space-designer/archive/refs/heads/main.zip
  2. Zip just the residential-space-designer/ folder (the one that contains SKILL.md).
  3. Upload it in Claude's settings under Capabilities → Skills, where custom skills are available on your plan.
  4. Start a chat and describe the room or home. Photos and floor plans work too.
  1. Python 3, nothing else to install. Check any layout and draw its plan: 
    python3 residential-space-designer/scripts/check_layout.py \
  residential-space-designer/examples/studio-apartment.json --plan
  2. Write your own layouts against templates/layout.schema.json. Units are metres, the origin is the south-west inner corner, and every element is a box.

Where the rules come from

Every number has a source tag.

Design patterns come from about 100 small-space renovations and 14 whole-home tours of Indian and Western houses and flats. Safety and clearance numbers come from codes and standards. Anything unconfirmed is tagged [std] and flagged to you.

[CPSC] [EN747]

Bunk and high-bed safety: guardrails, access, upper-bed age.

[IRC]

US residential code: escape windows, toilet and shower clearances, stairs, window falls.

[NKBA]

Kitchen and bath planning guidelines: aisles, work triangle, seating, fixtures.

[NP]

Neufert and Panero: bedside circulation, space in front of wardrobes.

[IES]

Lighting levels for general, reading and desk tasks.

[DI] [HT]

About 100 renovation case studies and 14 whole-home tours (Indian and Western), paraphrased, for patterns.

Not a substitute for a professional. Code values are minimums and vary by country and edition. Check local codes, and have structure, gas, electrics and waterproofing done or signed off by qualified people.
