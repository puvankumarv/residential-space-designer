# Room A: Pooja room (`17-a.json`)

## 1. Design brief
**Room:** `pooja`, 1.5 × 1.8 × 3.0 m, NE corner of the GF. Door on the W wall from the living room (double doors with a jaali or fluted panel, opening **out**). High ventilator on the N wall (sill 1.6 m). Dimensions are proposed, not measured.
**Occupants:** parents daily (the grandmother sits on a low stool), the whole family at festivals, 3–5 people standing at the door.
**Diagnosis (as briefed):** the pooja needs its own clean, quiet space in the NE. It needs ventilation for lamp and agarbatti smoke, storage so nothing sits out, and no strain for elder knees.
**Master decision:** a **compact walk-in pooja room**. The mandir is on the **east wall** on a 15 cm raised marble plinth, so the worshipper faces east. The doors open outward so the inside floor stays free for sitting.
**Rejected:**
- *Pooja unit in the living room:* you asked for a room, and a unit in the living loses quiet and sanctity.
- *Pooja under the stair:* commonly avoided (preference), and the stair is in the SW anyway.
- *Raising the whole room 15–20 cm:* a full step at the door is a trip hazard for elders. Only the altar plinth is raised.
- *Mandir on the north wall:* that faces the worshipper north. East-facing is the more common preference.

# Room B: Kitchen, SE (`17-b.json`)

## 1. Design brief
**Room:** `kitchen`, 3.0 × 3.6 × 3.0 m, SE corner of the GF. Door to dining on the N wall, door to the wet kitchen or utility on the W wall, window over the sink on the S wall. Proposed dimensions.
**Occupants:** grandmother (breakfast), couple (dinner), maid (cleaning). Daily tadka and pressure cooking.
**Diagnosis (as briefed):** heavy Indian cooking needs deep drawers for cookers and kadai, a ducted chimney and a tall pantry. Vessel washing and grinding should leave the dry kitchen. The cook faces east (Vastu).
**Master decision: L-shape.** The cooking run is on the **east wall** (hob in the SE, cook facing east), the sink run on the **south wall** under the window. The fridge and a crockery tower sit on the west wall, so the work triangle is closed on three sides and open to the dining door. Heavy washing goes to the adjacent **wet kitchen** [HT-I4, I7].
**Rejected:**
- *U-shape:* a third run on the west wall would block the utility door and squeeze the aisle.
- *Galley:* the two runs would face E and W, and the sink would lose the window.
- *Island:* needs 1.07 m on every side; 3.0 m of width can't give that.
- *Open kitchen to dining:* tadka fumes reach the living. Keep a door.

Work triangle: sink–hob 1.59 m, hob–fridge 2.43 m, fridge–sink 2.24 m. Total 6.3 m, inside the 1.2–2.7 m legs and 7.9 m total [NKBA].
