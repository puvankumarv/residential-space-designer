# 25 m² studio: work from home without your bed in every view

The goal is three moods in one room: kitchen by the door, a bright living and work zone in the middle, and a sleep alcove you can't see from the door, from the desk or on a video call. The move that does it is a **storage wall**. Wardrobe and closet office face the living side, and the bed's headboard sits against its back.

## 1. Design brief

**Room:** studio, 4.0 × 6.25 × 2.7 m (dimensions and ceiling assumed). Door: south wall, west corner. Windows: east wall at 2.4–3.6 m and 4.8–5.8 m from the south wall. Kitchen services: south wall (assumed).
**Occupants:** one adult who works from home.

**Diagnosis**
- **The bed is the first thing you see from the door** and the background of every call. There's no barrier between sleep and the rest of the room.
- **Work never ends.** The desk (or the dining table) stays in view all evening, so the room never switches to "home".
- **Loose furniture and little storage.** Clothes, work things and kitchen items compete for the same open shelves.
- **Smells reach the bed** if the kitchen sits next to the sleep area without a ducted hood.

**Master decision (room allocation and flow): a three-band plan split by a storage wall.**
1. **Band 1, by the door (south):** entry drop zone and a one-wall kitchen with a ducted hood. Cooking stays as far from the bed as possible.
2. **Band 2, middle, in the light from the first window:** a 2-seat sofa, a lift-top coffee table and a 2-seat table by the window.
3. **The storage wall** (0.6 m deep, 3.0 m long, 2.4 m high with fluted glass above for light and air):
   - **wardrobe** (1.8 m, sliding doors) facing the living side;
   - **closet office** (1.2 m desk inside, shelves and pinboard above, bi-fold doors) facing the living side. Close it at 6 pm and work disappears.
4. **Band 3, the sleep alcove (north):** the bed's headboard sits against the back of the storage wall. It is reached through a 1.0 m opening by the east wall with a blackout curtain, and has its own window (also its escape window).

The opening is at the far end from the front door, so the line of sight from the door goes past the bed, never into it. At the desk you face the storage wall with the window to your right (side light, no glare). The camera sees the sofa and the kitchen, never the bed.

**Rejected alternatives**
- *Wall bed (fold-up):* it hides the bed completely, but you'd fold it up every morning. Most people stop doing that within weeks, and then the bed is in full view again. It also gives up the calm, separate bedroom feel you're after.
- *Raised sleeping platform or loft:* a loft over a desk needs about 2.6 m of ceiling and puts the bed in view at eye level. A platform with storage still leaves the bed visible.
- *Curtain only (no storage wall):* the cheapest option, but it hides nothing when open, gives you no wardrobe and no office, and isn't a backdrop for calls.
- *Full-height solid partition with a door:* it closes off the second window's light from the living zone and makes two cramped rooms. The skill's divider hierarchy says to pick the lightest divider that solves the complaint. A storage wall plus a curtain does.

**Key trade-offs**
- The living zone gets one window. The sleep alcove keeps the other, because every sleeping area needs its own escape window.
- The bed's foot is at the north wall and you get in from either long side (0.6 m each side). A 1.6 m queen would leave only one usable side.
- The closet office is 1.2 m wide: enough for a laptop and one monitor. Two monitors need ≥ 1.4 m [std], which would make the wardrobe 1.6 m.

## 6. Safety notes

**Checker output** (`python3 scripts/check_layout.py 13.json --plan`):
```
0 error(s), 0 warning(s)
```
- **Escape window:** the alcove keeps its own operable window (sill 0.9 m ≤ 1.12 m [IRC]). Nothing taller than 1.0 m stands in front of it. If the flat is high up, fit window restrictors.
- The storage wall is joinery, not a wall. The curtain doesn't close the alcove off, so the bed's area still reaches its escape window.
- The desk outlet is exactly 1.0 m from the desk in the model. In practice a power rail inside the closet office, wired by an electrician, does the job.
- **[std] values, please verify:** desk width per person with two monitors (≥ 1.4 m).
- Check local codes, especially for a kitchen without a separate room, and the hood ducting.
