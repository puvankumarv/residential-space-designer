# Design brief template

## Intake questions (ask only what's missing; at most 5 per turn)
1. Which space: one room (which one?) or the whole home? For a whole home, a floor plan or a list of the rooms with rough sizes.
2. Room width × depth, and ceiling height? (If you only have a photo, I'll estimate from the door, about 0.8 × 2.0 m.)
3. Which wall has the door, and which has the window? Is there a bay window, a sloped ceiling, a beam or an AC unit?
4. Who uses it: how many people, their ages, and their relationship? For bedrooms: who sleeps where.
5. What happens here: sleep, study, WFH, cooking for how many, guests, gaming, hobbies, play?
6. What bothers you most about it now?
7. Fixed services: where are the water supply, drain or soil stack, gas, and the electrical panel? Which walls are load-bearing? (If unknown, I'll assume they're all load-bearing and the plumbing stays.)
8. Where is the home, and how do you live? For example Vastu, a pooja room, heavy daily cooking, elders, household staff, shoes-off.
9. Budget level and style (warm wood, minimal white, playful colour…)?

## Brief (output)

**Room:** type, W × D × H m, door on __, window on __, fixed services (estimated: yes/no)
**Occupants:** …
**Diagnosis:** the current problems (from the room module's diagnostics)
**Master decision** (sleep strategy / kitchen shape / fixture arrangement / seating focus): the choice, and why
**Rejected:** alternative, one-line reason (for each)
**Zones:** zone, purpose, owner
**Key trade-offs:** …
**Assumptions to confirm:** [std] values, estimated dimensions, structure
