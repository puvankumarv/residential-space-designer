## Clearances (stricter value is ideal, looser is the minimum for small rooms)

| Where | Ideal | Small-room minimum | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Beside a bed you get into | 0.70–0.90 | 0.60 | [NP] [BO] [IDE] |
| Main walkway | 0.90 | 0.60 | [BO] [IDE] |
| Squeeze point, not for regular use | — | 0.45 | [IDE] |
| In front of a hinged-door wardrobe | 0.90–1.05 | door leaf width + 0.10 | [NP] |
| In front of a sliding-door wardrobe or drawers | 0.75 | 0.60 | [NP] [IDE] |
| Desk chair zone | 0.75 | 0.60 | common practice |
| Foot of storage steps or ladder | 0.75 | 0.60 | [std] |
| Doorway clear width | 0.80 | 0.75 | [BO] |
| Door swing | The full leaf radius stays clear | — | ✔ checked |

Small rooms almost always need **sliding or bi-fold wardrobe doors** to meet these clearances [DI 3, 6, 11, 21, 24, 43, 70].

## Room heights

| Ceiling | What fits |
|---|---|
| < 2.4 | Floor or platform beds, half-height beds (mattress top about 0.8), offset designs |
| 2.4–2.6 | A loft with a den or sofa under it (≥ 1.0 clear); a loft with a desk only if the recommended headroom is relaxed to the minimum |
| ≥ 2.6 | A loft over a desk (1.5 + 0.25 + 0.84) |
| ≥ 2.9 | A loft with walk-in closet under it, or a classic bunk with comfortable lower headroom |
