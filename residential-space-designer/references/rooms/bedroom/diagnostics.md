# Diagnostic checklist

Name every symptom that applies before you design anything. Each symptom points to a usual fix. Numbers in brackets are case-study IDs from [video_notes.md](https://github.com/puvankumarv/residential-space-designer/tree/main/interior-design-research/video_notes.md) in the project repo.

| Symptom | Usual fix | Seen in |
|---|---|---|
| Bed in the middle of the room, walkways too narrow | Push the bed to a wall or corner, or raise it; free the centre | 20, 33, 67, 70, 96 |
| Wardrobe doors can't open (hit the bed, jam in a corner) | Sliding or bi-fold doors; move the wardrobe under a loft or behind the door | 3, 6, 11, 21, 24, 43, 70 |
| No room for a desk; desk squeezed at the bed foot | Desk under a loft, in the bay window, or pull-out | 6, 11, 18, 49, 59, 77 |
| Two nightstands taking wardrobe space | Wall niches or headboard shelves instead | 20, 55, 61, 68, 98 |
| Bay window used as a clutter shelf | Turn it into a desk, bed or seat with storage | 15, 20, 28, 53, 58, 80 |
| Classic bunk: no headroom on top, cramped below, no privacy | Offset, side-by-side or wardrobe-supported beds | 3, 10, 54, 75 |
| Shared kids' room: fighting, unequal space, no privacy | Mirrored equal zones, a divider, a personal den | 5, 51, 73, 94 |
| Lift-up under-bed storage is awkward, damp or mouldy | Wheeled drawers, pull-out cabinets, slatted ventilated base | 18, 22, 87 |
| Couple: one wardrobe for two; desk vs vanity conflict | Split storage by person; a split desk | 30, 61, 74, 97 |
| Micro-unit (≤ 4 m²): can't lie flat | Fold-up or pull-out bed; a professionally engineered extension | 12, 23, 32, 34, 47, 48, 57 |
| Exposed AC unit or pipes; nowhere to plug in | A stepped gypsum ceiling to hide the AC; pre-planned outlets | 6, 18, 24, 84, 96 |
| Clothes worn once pile up on the chair | Hooks or a "worn-once" section outside the wardrobe | 13, 37, 49, 61, 84 |
