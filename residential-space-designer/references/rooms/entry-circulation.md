# Entry, corridors and stairs

Source tags are explained in `../sources.md`. ✔ means `scripts/check_layout.py` checks it.

## Entry: the drop zone
Every home needs a spot within 1–2 steps of the front door for shoes, keys, bags and coats.
- A bench with a cushion; shoe compartments sized per season; a slipper gap at the floor; a mirror on the door or beside it [DI 4, 93].
- A full-height shoe cabinet with a recessed middle shelf for keys, or a floating cabinet with lighting underneath.
- Keep the door swing clear. ✔

| Handrail height | 0.86–0.97 m (34–38 in) | IRC R311.7 (verify locally) | |
| Guard openings | Must not pass a 0.10 m (4 in) sphere; the triangle at the stair side must not pass 0.15 m (6 in) | [IRC-S] | |

**Storage steps to a loft bed are furniture, not stairs.** They follow `safety.md`'s raised-bed rules, not these. Anything that's the main route between floors must meet the stair rules.

Under-stair space can hold drawers, a closet, a desk nook or a laundry bay [DI 13, 43, 65].

## Reveal order
Front door → drop zone → corridor storage → stair detail.
