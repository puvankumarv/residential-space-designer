# Balcony and laundry

Source tags are explained in `../sources.md`.

## Master decision: the wet-utility stack
Put the washer and dryer where water supply and drainage already exist. Usually that's the balcony, the bathroom or the kitchen wall.

- **Laundry tower:** a full-height cabinet around the washer and dryer stack, built to hide the pipes, with shelves above [DI 9, 38].
- **Drying:** a retractable ceiling drying rack [DI 26, 38].
- **Robot-vacuum bay** in the base of the cabinet [DI 9].
- **Utility sink** next to the washer if there's room.

## Rules
| Rule | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Space in front of a front-loader | about 1.0 m to load and kneel | [std] |
| Balcony guard height | Follow the local code (commonly 0.91–1.07 m); never lower it with furniture someone could climb | [std] |
| Balcony loading | Don't add heavy built-ins, planters or water features without a structural check | [std] |
| Outdoor electrics | Weatherproof sockets with ground-fault protection, fitted by an electrician | [std] |
| Drainage | Keep the floor drain clear; slope towards it | [DI 9] |

## Reveal order
Door to the balcony → laundry tower → drying rack → leisure corner (if any).
