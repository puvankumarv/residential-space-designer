# Home office

Source tags are explained in `../sources.md`. Desks are also checked in bedrooms and studios.

## Master decision: desk orientation
- Put the window **to the side** of the screen, not behind it (glare on the screen) or behind you (glare and a backlit face on video calls) [std].
- Keep a clean, closable background for video calls: a cabinet wall or a curtain.
- If the office shares a bedroom or living room, use a cabinet desk that closes or a curtain so work can be hidden after hours.

## Storage moves
Overhead cabinets above the desk [DI 3, 48]; a pegboard or pinboard; a mobile drawer unit under the desk; a closet office (desk inside a wardrobe with bi-fold doors).
