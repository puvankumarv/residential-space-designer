# Design brief template

## Intake questions (ask only what's missing; at most 5 per turn)
1. Room width × depth, and ceiling height? (If you only have a photo, I'll estimate from the door, about 0.8 × 2.0 m.)
2. Which wall has the door, and which has the window? Is there a bay window, a sloped ceiling, a beam or an AC unit?
3. Who sleeps here: how many people, their ages, and siblings or a couple?
4. What else happens here: study, WFH, gaming, makeup, hobbies, play?
5. What bothers you most about the room now?
6. Do you know which walls are load-bearing? (If not, I'll assume all of them are.)
7. Budget level and style (warm wood, minimal white, playful colour…)?

**Room:** W × D × H m, door on __, window on __ (estimated: yes/no)
**Occupants:** …
**Diagnosis:** the current problems (from `references/diagnostics.md`)
**Sleep strategy:** chosen branch, and why
**Rejected:** alternative, one-line reason (for each)
**Zones:** zone, purpose, owner
**Key trade-offs:** …
**Assumptions to confirm:** [std] values, estimated dimensions, structure
