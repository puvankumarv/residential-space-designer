# Storage module catalog

Each module maps to a layout element `type` (in backticks). Sizes are in `dimensions.md`.

| Module | `type` | Rule of thumb | Seen in |
|---|---|---|---|
| Floor-to-ceiling wardrobe | `wardrobe` | Quilts and seasonal items on top, daily hanging in the middle, drawers at the bottom; sliding doors in tight rooms | almost every case |
| Long-hang section | `wardrobe` attr | Reserve one column for dresses and coats | 6, 15, 17, 28, 80 |
| "Worn-once" hooks | `hooks` | Outside the wardrobe or on a bed side | 13, 37, 49, 61, 84 |
| Storage steps | `steps` | Each box step is a drawer or bookshelf; split drawers by person | 2, 11, 21, 62, 79, 87, 97 |
| Storage staircase | `steps` | A full stair cabinet at the bed foot; den or quilts under it | 13, 43, 65, 84 |
| Under-bed drawers | `drawers` | 3–5 wheeled drawers or pull-out cabinets; avoid lift-up lids | 3, 10, 24, 28, 53 |
| Wall niche | `niche` | Replaces a nightstand; **non-load-bearing walls only** | 2, 18, 45, 69, 82 |
| Overhead cabinet | `cabinet` (z > 1.8) | Above a desk, doorway or headboard | 3, 48, 60, 68 |
| Behind-door cabinet | `cabinet` | Shallow (0.25–0.35), full height | 54, 55, 70, 75, 94 |
| Platform at the bed foot | `platform` | Storage plus steps, a sofa on top | 17, 24, 52 |
| Bay-window unit | `desk` / `bed` / `seat` | Extend a desk top 0.30–0.40 into the room for legroom; curtains inside the recess | 20, 55, 58, 74 |
| Pinboard or blackboard | `board` | Wall organization at the kids' desk | about 25 cases |
| Walk-in closet | `closet` | A gypsum false wall or curtain at the bed head or foot, or under a loft | 39, 45, 70, 76, 83 |
| Divider cabinet | `divider` | Storage on both faces between two beds | 10, 75, 94 |

## Storage volume heuristic
Aim for at least 1.0 m of wardrobe width per adult and 0.6–0.8 m per child, plus drawers. If you can't fit that, say what is short and suggest an overhead cabinet or under-bed drawers to make it up.
