# Other rooms (secondary)

This skill is about bedrooms. Use these short rule sets only when the user asks about an adjoining space.

- **Bathroom** [14, 31, 56, 63, 90]: concealed cistern with a wall-hung toilet; a linear drain; a glass wet/dry split; a floor-to-ceiling mirror cabinet with a towel slot; a floating or open-bottom vanity (no dust corners); a pull-out step for kids; waterproof twice and run a 48 h water test. An electrician does all wet-zone wiring.
- **Balcony / laundry** [9, 26, 38]: a full-height laundry tower built around the pipes; a retractable ceiling drying rack; a robot-vacuum bay.
- **Entryway** [4, 93]: a bench with a cushion; shoe compartments sized per season; a slipper gap at floor level; a mirror on the door.
- **Kitchen** [66]: a corner single-bowl sink; extend the counter under a lowered window sill; stacked pull-out baskets.
- **Living room** [26]: a corner platform sofa with a drawer base instead of a loose sofa.
