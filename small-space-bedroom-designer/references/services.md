# Services

- **Outlets:** put one within reach of every bed (including raised beds), every desk (two or more, or a power rail [37]) and every niche. Plan them before cladding [16, 49, 74]. In the layout, list them under `services` with wall, offset and height. The checker warns when a bed or desk has no outlet within 1.0 m.
- **Lighting [IES]:** general lighting at 50–150 lux, desk and reading tasks at 300–500 lux, warm 2700–3000 K in the evening. Each raised bed gets its own reading light. Motion-sensor lights at partitions and arches [10, 97]; low-voltage LED strips in steps for night safety [62]; a lit headboard [89].
- **AC and fan-coil:** hide them in a stepped ("double-eyelid") gypsum ceiling, and keep the airflow off sleepers' faces [6, 18, 24, 84, 96].
- **Ventilation:** slatted bed bases [7, 10, 18]; a gap above built-in beds [40]; moisture-resistant panels in damp climates [44]; never seal a mattress over a closed box.
- **Acoustics:** insulation boards around the bed [53]; a soundproof partition between siblings [10].
- **Switches:** two-way switches at the door and at the bed, including at upper-bed height.
