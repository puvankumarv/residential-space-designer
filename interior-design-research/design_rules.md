# Small-Space Bedroom Design Rules (draft)

Distilled from 95 narrated videos in [`video_notes.md`](video_notes.md). Numbers in brackets are note IDs. This is the seed for the skill's `references/` files. Rules are written in our own words. Dimensions marked **[std]** come from common practice, not the videos, and must be checked against local codes.

## 1. Design procedure (every video follows this script)

1. **Intake:** room size (m²), ceiling height, window/bay window, door position, occupants (count, ages, genders, relationship), activities (sleep, study, WFH, gaming, makeup, hobbies), storage volume.
2. **Diagnose the problem:** the videos repeatedly name the same failures (§2).
3. **Choose a sleep strategy:** this is the master decision (§3).
4. **Zone the room:** sleep / study / dressing / storage / leisure, plus privacy per occupant (§4).
5. **Fill every vertical surface:** storage modules (§5).
6. **Services:** outlets, switches, lighting, AC, ventilation (§6).
7. **Safety check** (§7).
8. **Reveal:** a zone-by-zone walkthrough. This order is also the camera path for the 3D video.

| Symptom | Seen in |

## 3. Sleep strategy decision tree

```
Occupants = 1
├─ room ≥ ~8 m² and no study need     → platform bed against wall / in bay window, drawers under [18, 70]
├─ room 5–9 m², needs desk + storage  → LOFT BED (raised ~1.6–1.8 m [std]) with desk/closet/den under [1, 6, 16, 22, 27]
│     variants: pull-out desk under loft [6, 16, 64, 84]; under-bed walk-in closet [22, 42, 98];
│               under-bed gaming den with hidden door [1, 7, 41, 78, 92]
├─ room 5–8 m², low ceiling           → HALF-HEIGHT bed (~0.8 m) + storage beneath + desk alongside [21, 24 (raised 80 cm)]
└─ micro-unit ≤ 4 m²                  → fold-up wall bed [36], pull-out sofa-bed from drawers [12, 23],
                                         or cantilever a steel frame outside the window to gain bed length [34, 48, 57]

Occupants = 2 kids
├─ avoid classic stacked bunk (headroom/privacy complaints) [10, 54, 75]
├─ side-by-side raised platform + central divider cabinet [10, 51, 75, 94]
├─ offset L-bunk in a corner (upper and lower don't overlap) [54]
├─ upper bed on top of back-to-back wardrobes; lower bed by window [73]
└─ classic bunk acceptable only with adult-height clearance below [42]

Occupants = 3–12 kids → steel-frame multi-bed system, beds on vertical columns, box-stairs as drawers,
                        long shared desk row, per-child drawer sections [8, 62, 79, 86]

Couple → raised platform with storage + his/her drawers in steps; split desk (WFH + vanity) [30, 74, 83, 87, 97]
```

## 4. Zoning & privacy rules

- **Zone dividers, from lightest to heaviest:** sheer curtain → arched opening [15, 52, 89, 97] → curved partition [65, 75] → frosted/fluted glass [8, 86, 94] → divider cabinet [10, 75, 94] → gypsum / fire-resistant partition [3, 5, 7, 73] → partition + hinged door [54].
- **Fairness for shared rooms:** mirrored, equal layouts: same wardrobe, desk and storage per child [5, 51, 75, 94].
- **Gender separation:** older mixed-sex siblings get a partition plus a door or glass panel [54, 86].
- **Keep the center open:** a shared activity area, "room to dance" [8, 67, 68].
- **Privacy for teens:** an enclosed den with a hidden or secret door [1, 5, 7, 41, 69, 78, 82, 92].
- **Parent seat:** desks wide enough for a parent to sit alongside [20, 40, 44].

## 5. Storage module catalog

| Module | Rule of thumb | Seen in |
|---|---|---|
| Floor-to-ceiling wardrobe | Quilts/seasonal on top, daily hanging in the middle, drawers (underwear/socks) at the bottom | ~every video |

| "Worn-once" hooks | Outside hooks/section for clothes that aren't dirty yet | 13, 37, 49, 61, 84 |

| Storage staircase | Full stair cabinet at the bed foot, den/quilts under it | 13, 43, 65, 84 |
| Under-bed | Wheeled drawers (3–5) or pull-out cabinets; avoid lift-up lids | 3, 10, 24, 28, 53 |
| Wall niche | Recess replaces the nightstand (non-load-bearing walls only) | 2, 18, 45, 69, 82 |
| Overhead cabinets | Above desks / doorways / headboards | 3, 48, 60, 68 |

## 6. Services

- **Pre-plan outlets and switches at the bedside, desk and niche** before cladding [16, 49, 74].
- **Hide the AC / fan-coil unit** in a stepped ("double-eyelid") gypsum ceiling [6, 18, 24, 84, 96].
- **Lighting:** motion-sensor lights at partitions and arches [10, 97]; hidden LED strips in steps [62]; a lit headboard [89].
- **Ventilation:** slatted bed bases [7, 10, 18]; a ventilation gap above the bed [40]; moisture-resistant panels [44].
- **Soundproofing:** insulation boards around the bed [53]; a soundproof partition [10].
- **Track-mounted power rail** at the desk [37].

## 7. Safety rules (these must be validated by the MCP)

- A **guardrail** on every raised bed, and **padded wall panels** beside beds [many].
- A ladder or steps with **anti-slip** treads [16, 47, 60]; protective ropes on steps in large multi-kid rooms [79].
- **Headroom** (videos only say "won't bump head", so use these [std] values):
  - **Under a loft:** ≥ 1.5 m for sitting at a desk. Adult walk-through needs ≥ 1.9 m [42].
  - **Above the upper mattress:** ≥ 0.84 m recommended, 0.76 m minimum. These values are superseded by the sourced versions in `residential-space-designer/references/safety.md`.
- **Structure:** frames are galvanized square steel, welded, on vertical columns, and fixed with expansion bolts [80, 82].
  - **⚠ Never cut niches into load-bearing walls.** Videos 69 and 82 note this; video 86 claims to carve one, so flag it.
- Don't put the guardrail and ladder on the window side without window railings [42].

## 8. Materials vocabulary (for BOM and render materials)

Galvanized square steel tube (frame) · eco-friendly wood panels / solid wood / wood veneer (cladding) · gypsum board (partitions, ceilings) · fire-resistant board · slatted bed base · upholstered/padded wall panels · frosted, fluted or tempered glass · aluminum ("aerospace") panels for wet areas and cantilevers · marble countertops · large-format tiles.

## 9. Non-bedroom rule sets (secondary)

- **Bathroom** [14, 31, 56, 63, 90]: concealed cistern + wall-hung toilet; linear drain with 2 outlets; glass wet/dry split; floor-to-ceiling mirror cabinet with a towel slot; floating or open-bottom vanity (no dust corners); kids' pull-out step; waterproof twice + 48 h water test.
- **Balcony / laundry** [9, 26, 38]: full-height laundry tower around the pipes; retractable ceiling drying rack; robot-vacuum bay.
- **Entryway** [4, 93]: bench + cushion; shoe compartments sized per season; slipper gap at the floor; mirror on the door.
- **Kitchen** [66]: corner diamond single-bowl sink; extend the counter under a lowered window sill; stacked pull-out baskets.
- **Living** [26]: corner platform sofa with a drawer base instead of a loose sofa.
