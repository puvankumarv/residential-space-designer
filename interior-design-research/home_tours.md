## Cross-cutting patterns (feed into the skill)

1. **Privacy by floor (vertical zoning).** Public and elder/guest rooms on the ground floor; private family rooms upstairs; hosting or wellness on the top or bottom level [HT-I1, I2, I4, I5, W4].
2. **A double-height or courtyard "heart"** that the other rooms overlook, bringing light into deep plots [HT-I1, I2, I3, I5, W3, W5].
3. **Day-lighting deep, crowded plots:** skylights over the living, stair and bathrooms; glass-block ceilings; openings on the free sides [HT-I3, I5].
4. **Arrival sequence:** separate pedestrian and car entries; don't let parking dominate the frontage; raised plinth and compound wall for privacy [HT-I1, I2, I4, W5].
5. **Elders:** a ground-floor room, near the pooja room, with no level changes; a lift in multi-storey homes [HT-I1, I4, I7].
6. **Indian kitchen program:** dry (show/family) kitchen plus a wet/work kitchen or utility; storage planned from the utensil list; deep drawers for big vessels; dishwasher beside the sink [HT-I4, I6, I7].
7. **Pooja room/unit** as a sacred, often raised space; can combine with a library or reading seat [HT-I1, I2, I5, I6].
8. **Swings** (oonjal/jhoola) in a courtyard, foyer, verandah or living [HT-I1, I2, I3, I5, I8].
9. **Transformable rooms:** sliding partitions or curtains carve a gym, theatre or guest room out of the living [HT-I4, W1].
10. **Stairs as storage and libraries** [HT-I3, W3, W6].
11. **Plan for growth:** terraces and lofts that become rooms later [HT-W3, W6].
12. **Indoor–outdoor:** every room opens to a garden, courtyard, deck or balcony [HT-I5, W5, W6].
13. **Respect what exists:** keep original floors, structures and heirlooms; reuse furniture [HT-I6, W2, W3, W5].
14. **Climate response:** double glazing against the west sun, sloping roofs, verandahs and shutters, cross-ventilation transoms [HT-I1, I2, W2].
